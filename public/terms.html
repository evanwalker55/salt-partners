Salt Partners program

Updated October 23, 2026.

Your account and company

Register using your own mobile number and the company you work with. Your company membership must be approved by Simply Salt or created using its invitation. Keep your sign-in codes private. Contact Evan if you change companies; opening another company’s link does not change your membership.

Customer referrals

Only submit a customer’s information and delivery booking with their permission. Confirm the address, phone number, and delivery time with them. Do not submit duplicate, fabricated, or unauthorized bookings. Simply Salt performs the delivery and manages the customer’s service.

Technician rewards

The current program pays the individual technician $25 for an eligible referred delivery after completion is confirmed. Pending bookings are not earned rewards. Cancelled or uncompleted deliveries do not qualify. Your account shows pending, earned, and paid amounts separately.

Rewards are paid by Simply Salt using the payout method and recipient details you provide. Check those details carefully. Saving a payment handle does not verify its ownership or automatically transfer money. Contact Evan about a missing reward or a disputed attribution.

Free stickers

Approved partnership stickers are provided to partner technicians at no charge, including standard fulfillment arranged by Simply Salt. Requests are reviewed by the owner; submitting a request does not send a paid order to a printer. Your request retains the design version shown at submission.

AI tank mockups illustrate how a partnership sticker could look on a tank. They are not print files or evidence of a customer installation. Only artwork approved in the sticker request screen is used for fulfillment.

Help and account closure

Contact Evan at (317) 969-0174 for company changes, booking corrections, reward questions, or shipment help. You can delete your account from its settings. See the privacy policy for how outstanding service and payment records are handled.
