Delete your Salt Partners account

You can request account deletion here even if you no longer have the mobile app.

  1. Sign in on this website using the phone number on your account.
  2. Open Account → Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm. If asked, verify your phone again to protect your account.

Your active sign-in, phone number, and profile are removed and device sessions are revoked. Limited information needed to complete outstanding service, sticker shipments, unpaid rewards, or legitimate accounting reconciliation may remain until that work is settled. See our privacy policy.

If you cannot access your old number or need help completing deletion, email evan@simplysalt.com or text (317) 969-0174 and ask to delete your Salt Partners account. Simply Salt will verify ownership before removing an account.
