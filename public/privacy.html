Privacy at Salt Partners

Updated November 1, 2026. Salt Partners is operated by Simply Salt for its partner companies and their technicians.

Information we use

We use your name, verified mobile number, company membership, selected payout method and payout username, phone number, or email to operate your partner account and arrange referral rewards. We do not collect bank passwords, payment PINs, or credit card numbers.

When you book a customer’s delivery, we collect their name, address, phone, optional email, water type, delivery selection, and driver instructions. Only enter these details with the customer’s permission. We also record which company and technician referred the booking, its Housecall Pro job reference, delivery status, and reward history.

For free sticker requests, we collect the recipient, shipping address, quantity, approved design version, request status, and fulfillment notes. Account security records include hashed session identifiers, verification timestamps, and hashed rate-limit identifiers. Hosting and communications providers also process connection information such as IP addresses and service logs to operate and protect their services.

Services that process information

Netlify hosts the app and its private records. Twilio Verify processes mobile numbers and sign-in codes. Our scheduling automation, hosted on Railway, processes the customer’s name, service address, and ZIP code to find delivery availability. Housecall Pro processes customer and delivery information. Simply Salt uses your chosen payment service to send rewards outside this app. Shipping details may be shared with the printer or carrier needed to fulfill your sticker request.

AI-generated tank images are illustrative marketing mockups. Customer addresses, customer photos, payout details, and account records are not sent to an AI image service by this app.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising or tracking.

Who can see your records

Technicians can access their own account, referrals, and sticker requests. Simply Salt’s owner can manage companies, memberships, deliveries, rewards, and fulfillment. The public company gallery shows company names, logos, and selected partnership photos; it does not show technician accounts or customer details.

Sample workspace

The optional sample workspace uses temporary records in your device’s memory. Enter sample information only. Sample bookings, payout details, sticker requests, and deletions are not sent to Simply Salt or its providers. Exiting or reloading clears those records.

Storage and deletion

We keep active account records while the account is used. You can delete your account in Account → Delete my account, or use the web deletion page without reinstalling the app. We remove your sign-in access, mobile number, and profile details from the active technician account and revoke device sessions.

Limited records needed to complete outstanding deliveries, sticker shipments, or unpaid rewards remain until that work is settled. The app removes retained payout destinations after all referral rewards are settled and shipping details after a deleted account’s sticker request is fulfilled or cancelled. Closed referral records are reduced to accounting and attribution references; customer contact details and driver notes are removed from this app. Records required for legitimate accounting or dispute purposes may be retained separately. Contact Simply Salt to ask what information remains and request removal where it is no longer needed. Customer service records for an independently booked delivery may remain in Housecall Pro to provide that service.

Device features

The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location. Sign-in cookies keep you authenticated. The mobile app may offer your device’s share sheet and a short confirmation vibration. These features do not send a message without your action.
