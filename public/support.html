Help with Salt Partners

Salt Partners is Simply Salt’s referral app for technicians at partner plumbing, home care, and water treatment companies.

Contact Simply Salt

Email evan@simplysalt.com or text (317) 969-0174 for help signing in, joining your company, correcting a booking, checking a reward, or requesting stickers.

Include your referral or sticker request reference if available. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, payment PINs, or bank login details.

Sign-in and company access

Use your own US mobile number. We text a six-digit code. Returning technicians open their saved company account automatically. New accounts need an invitation or owner approval. If you change companies or lose access to your number, contact Simply Salt.

Referral rewards and stickers

Rewards become payable after a delivery is confirmed complete. Simply Salt sends payments separately using your saved payout destination. Sticker requests are free and fulfilled by Simply Salt after review.

Account deletion

Open Account → Delete my account, or use the account deletion page. If you cannot sign in, email Simply Salt to request deletion; we will verify account ownership.

Explore before joining

Choose “Explore sample workspace” on the app’s opening screen to try the main features without signing in. Use sample information only. Those actions do not send messages, create deliveries, order stickers, or transfer money.
