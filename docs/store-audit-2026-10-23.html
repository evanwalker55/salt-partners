Draft data disclosures

The app collects account names, verified phone numbers, company membership and user identifiers; optional payout destinations and reward/payment records; customer names, phones, addresses, optional email and service notes; sticker recipient/shipping details; and operational activity/security records. Primary purpose: app functionality, service fulfillment, account security, and reward administration. No advertising/tracking SDK is installed. Payout destinations are financial data even though no card or bank password is collected.

Before finalizing either store form, verify Netlify/Twilio/Housecall Pro logs, retention and subprocessors, classify optional versus required data in the actual form, and review the archive privacy report. Service providers and fulfillment/payment recipients must be described accurately; a service-provider exception in Google’s definition of “sharing” does not mean no data leaves the app.
